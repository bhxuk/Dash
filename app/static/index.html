Connect Home Assistant

Dash controls your lights, Aqara cameras and Google/Nest devices through Home Assistant, so add them there first. Dash stores the token on your server and never sends it to the browser.

How to get a token and add your devices
  1. Token: open Home Assistant → click your name (bottom left) → Security → Long-lived access tokens → Create token. Name it “Dash”, copy it and paste it above.
  2. Google Nest speakers, Nest Hubs and Chromecasts: Settings → Devices & services. They are usually discovered automatically as Google Cast; click Configure. For Nest thermostats and cameras add the Google Nest integration.
  3. Aqara cameras and hubs (G2H Pro, G3, G5, M2, M3): add with the HomeKit Device integration using the HomeKit code on the device. The Aqara M3 also works over Matter. For a smoother live view, turn on RTSP LAN preview in the Aqara Home app and add the rtsp:// address with the Generic Camera integration.
  4. Smart bulbs: add the matching integration (Philips Hue, TP-Link Kasa/Tapo, LIFX, WiZ, Govee, Yeelight, Tuya, IKEA, Matter…). Bulbs you control in the Google Home app can also be linked through that brand's integration.
  5. Back in Dash, open Smart home. Star ★ a device to pin it to your overview.

Keep a copy of your space

Export your links, categories, notes, tasks, appearance and smart home favourites as a JSON file. Import it to restore or move your dashboard. Your Home Assistant token is not included.

Snapshots are saved to data/backups/ on the server. Imports and updates create one automatically.

No login is enabled. Keep Dash on your trusted home network; anyone who can reach it can edit the dashboard and switch your devices.
