YOUR SERVER. YOUR RULES.

Your dashboard stays
on your network.

One small Docker container. No login screen, no cloud database and no Docker socket. Your links and preferences stay in a persistent data folder on your server.

Designed for a trusted LAN. Everyone who can reach Dash can edit it. Keep its port private.

A FEW COMMANDS. A NEW START.

Meet your new start page.

You’ll need a Linux server, Docker Compose and Git. Dash builds locally and runs on your LAN.

TERMINAL
git clone https://github.com/bhxuk/Dash.git
cd Dash
bash install.sh
Open http://<your-server-ip>:8088
01

Install on your server.The installer sets your LAN address and user permissions, then starts Docker.

02

Make yourself at home.Add your links or use the homelab starter pack. Pick your colours and categories.

03

Keep it fresh.New releases install automatically, with a backup before every update.
